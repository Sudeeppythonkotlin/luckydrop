Play in 4 steps

  1. Log in with Google.One tap. No payment, no card.
  2. Pick your number.One letter and four digits, like A7381.
  3. Claim it.If nobody has taken that number today, it's yours.
  4. Check at 8 PM.The winner appears on the Winners page.

The rules

  1. It's free. You never pay to enter or to receive a prize. Nobody from LuckyDrop will ever ask you for money.
  2. 18+ and in India. You must be 18 or older and live in India.
  3. One ticket per person, per day. You claim with your Google account, and each account gets one ticket a day.
  4. 10:00 to 11:00 AM IST. Tickets can only be claimed in this window.
  5. Every number is unique. If someone already holds your number today, pick another one.
  6. One day, one draw. A ticket only counts for the draw on the day you claimed it.
  7. Random winner. A computer picks one ticket at random from all tickets claimed that day. Every ticket has the same chance.
  8. Winner is told two ways. The number is shown on the Winners page, and we email the winner at their Google account address.
  9. Claiming a prize. The winner replies within 7 days and confirms their identity. The prize is paid to the winner's own bank account or UPI. Where the law requires tax to be deducted from a prize, it is deducted before payment.
  10. Play fair. Bots, extra accounts or other tricks lead to disqualification.
  11. Changes. If we change or pause the game, we'll say so on the site.

Is it really fair?

Yes. Nobody can buy extra chances, and nobody on our team picks winners by hand. The draw is random, and every result stays on the Winners page for anyone to check.

Questions

Why do I need a Google account?

So each person gets one ticket a day. We only use your name and email to run the game.

Can I choose the same number every day?

Yes, as long as nobody else has taken it that day.

I missed the 10 to 11 AM window.

Come back tomorrow at 10 AM. A new Drop opens every day.

I won. What now?

Check your email. We'll write to the address on your Google account. Reply within 7 days to claim your prize.

How does LuckyDrop pay for prizes?

Through ads and product links on the site. You never pay.
